Privacy Policy

Effective: September 8, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how SSoW Vault handles information when you use the website. SSoW Vault is a fan-made community tool for displaying publicly available Secret Society of Whales NFT information.

Information You Provide

SSoW Vault allows users to enter a public Ethereum wallet address in order to display Secret Society of Whales NFTs associated with that address.

Ethereum wallet addresses and blockchain ownership information are publicly available on the blockchain.

Temporary Data Processing

Wallet lookup results may be temporarily cached to improve performance, reduce unnecessary requests to third-party services, and provide a faster experience.

Limited technical information, such as an IP address, may also be temporarily processed for security, abuse prevention, and rate limiting.

Wallet Security

SSoW Vault does not request seed phrases, private keys, recovery phrases, or wallet passwords.

SSoW Vault is a read-only experience and does not connect to a user's wallet or initiate blockchain transactions.

Third-Party Services

SSoW Vault relies on third-party services to operate and retrieve publicly available blockchain and NFT information. These may include services such as OpenSea, Vercel, Upstash, and Google-hosted resources.

These third-party providers may process technical information according to their own privacy policies and terms.

Cookies and Advertising

SSoW Vault does not currently use advertising cookies or behavioral advertising technologies.

If analytics, advertising, or additional tracking technologies are added in the future, this Privacy Policy may be updated accordingly.

Sale of Personal Information

SSoW Vault does not sell personal information or public wallet addresses for advertising purposes.

Data Retention

Information used for caching and rate limiting is intended to be retained only temporarily for the operation and security of the service.

Children's Privacy

SSoW Vault is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as the website, its features, or its third-party services change. The effective date above will be updated when material changes are made.
